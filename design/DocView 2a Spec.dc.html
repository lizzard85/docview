1 · Title bar & tabs
Tabs always show, even when there is only one. Empty title-bar space is the drag area; double-click maximises or restores. Tabs shrink to 112px, then the strip scrolls with a fade and an "all tabs" menu appears. Close turns terracotta on hover.

2 · Responsive toolbar
Collapse order as the window narrows: line count → Copy/Open labels and "recognized" → History, Compare, Editor, Save, Convert into "…" → Copy into "…" → Format/Wrap/Line no. into "View ▾" → Open and the Paste label. The language pill, the panel toggle, Paste and Settings never leave the bar. Hidden actions keep their shortcuts.

5 · Notice bars & update bar
Notice bars sit inside the top of the code card (margin 10px 10px 0) and push the code down. There are three tones: info (sage), warning/error (terracotta) and update (neutral, full width below the toolbar). There is one bar at a time per tab; the newest replaces the old one.

6 · Toast & tooltip
Both are inverse-coloured (text colour as background). The toast sits bottom-centre, 20px above the edge, for 2.2s with a 160ms fade/slide. The tooltip appears after 500ms, 8px from its target, with the current shortcut in a key chip.

9 · Compare
Compare opens a menu (left) and then replaces the code card and panel with two aligned panes. Removed lines are terracotta, added lines sage, and changed lines neutral with inline word highlights. The current change gets a 3px accent bar. F7 / Shift+F7 or the arrows move between changes.

11 · Splitter & scrollbars
The 12px gap between the cards is the splitter. On hover a 4×36 grip pill appears (col-resize cursor). Minimum widths: code 320px, panel 260px. Width is remembered per panel type, and double-click resets it. Scrollbars are overlay pills inset 3px: 6px wide at rest, 10px on hover.
